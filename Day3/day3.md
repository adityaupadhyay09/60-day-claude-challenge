# Day 3 - Role-Based Prompting

## What is Role-Based Prompting?

Role-Based Prompting means assigning a specific role to AI before asking a question.

Example:
"You are a Growth Marketing Expert."

This helps AI respond from that expert's perspective.

## Why It Matters

Assigning a role provides context and helps AI generate more relevant, accurate, and useful responses.

## Without Role Prompt

Question:
How can I improve user retention for my app?

Answer:
Focus on improving user experience, fixing bugs, and collecting feedback.

## With Role Prompt

Role:
You are a Growth Marketing Expert.

Question:
How can I improve user retention for my app?

Answer:
Use onboarding optimization, user segmentation, retention campaigns, behavioral nudges, and A/B testing.

## My Biggest Observation

The question stayed the same, but the perspective completely changed.

Role-Based Prompting transformed a generic answer into an expert-level response.

## Benefits

1. More Relevant Answers
2. Higher Quality & Depth
3. Save Time & Effort
